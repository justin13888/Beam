## Outcome

Every Android setting and admin action does what its label says, and reports when it fails:
- Toggling software decoding re-sends the device profile.
- The download-over-cellular preference reaches the download manager.
- A failed "sign out everywhere" says so instead of claiming success.
- Refused or failed admin mutations show an error.

## Findings

- **android-TEST-010** `beam-android/app/src/main/kotlin/dev/beam/android/MainViewModel.kt:52-57`. `allowSoftware` is read once with `first()`, so toggling it never re-sends the profile. `setRequiresUnmeteredNetwork` has no caller; `grep -rn 'setRequiresUnmeteredNetwork('` finds only its definitions (`DownloadRepository.kt:56, 117`, `BeamDownloadManager.kt:180`).
- **android-TEST-009** `beam-android/feature/settings/.../SettingsViewModel.kt:178`. `signOutEverywhere` swallows a failure and still marks the viewer signed out. The core documents that failure as a real error (`beam-client-core/src/ffi.rs:1753-1756`). `SettingsTest.kt:117-141` never sets `failWith`.
- **android-TEST-019** `beam-android/feature/admin/.../AdminViewModel.kt:184,220`. When `setUserDisabled` or `deleteLibrary` fails (e.g. `Forbidden`), the error is swallowed with no message. `deleteLibrary` has no test.
- **android-TEST-015** `beam-android/feature/auth/.../AuthViewModel.kt:200,235,58`. `onDeviceLoginCancelled` is called in a test with no assertion after it (`AuthViewModelTest.kt:322`). `forget()` and `connect(existingServerId)` are untested.

## Decisions taken

These are the run's decisions under the autonomy contract. Nobody has reviewed them.

1. Make the settings take effect, or remove them.
   Taken: make them take effect. Both are user-visible settings that already exist, so this is a defect fix, not a feature.
   Rejected: removing the toggles. That takes away a control the app already offers.
   Reverses: delete the two preferences and their UI.
