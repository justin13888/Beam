---
title: Resume and history
description: Beam remembers where you stopped, without you saving anything.
---

Beam records your position as you watch. There is nothing to press — it reports periodically
during playback, so closing the tab, losing power or having the browser crash still leaves you
close to where you were.

## Continue watching

Titles you are partway through appear in a continue-watching row on the home page, so the thing
you are most likely to want next is the first thing you see. Finish something and it drops off
the row.

## Resuming

Reopening a partly-watched title picks up where you stopped rather than starting from the
beginning. If you would rather start over, seek back — the resume point is a starting position,
not a restriction.

## Watch history

The history page lists what you have watched and when, across every title, in one place.

## Two things that surprise people

- **Your position is yours alone.** Every user's progress is tracked separately, so watching
  something on a shared server does not affect anyone else's resume point.
- **Position is tracked per file, not per title.** Switching to a different quality version of
  the same title means switching to a different file, so your place may not carry across. Pick
  the source you want before settling in — see [Watching](/using/watching/).
