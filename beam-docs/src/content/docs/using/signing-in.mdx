Beam has no passwords. Signing in hands you to the identity provider the server's administrator
configured — the same account you use for other services there — and brings you back.

There is **no account to create**. The first time Beam sees your identity, it creates your user
record automatically.

## What you will not find

- No sign-up form.
- No "forgot password" link. Beam never had your password to reset.
- No way to change your email or display name in Beam — those come from your identity provider.

If you need any of those, they belong to your identity provider, not Beam.

## When sign-in does not work

**You are sent back to the app still signed out.** Your browser most likely discarded the
short-lived cookie that ties the round trip together. Blocking third-party cookies can do it, and
so can reaching Beam over plain HTTP when it expects HTTPS. Start again from the app rather than
reloading the callback URL, which is single-use.

**"This account has been disabled."** Your identity was verified, but an administrator has
disabled your Beam account. Nothing you can change will fix it — ask them.

**A 503 mentioning OIDC.** The server's identity provider settings are incomplete or its provider
is unreachable. That is a server-side problem; report it to whoever runs it.

<Aside type="note">
Both of the first two produce an error page rather than a redirect loop. The
[error reference](/reference/errors/) explains what each status means, and
[Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/) walks through the fixes.
</Aside>

## Admin access

If you are expecting an admin area and cannot see one, your account is not an admin. Beam decides
that solely from a claim your identity provider asserts, and it re-checks at **every** sign-in. If
someone has just granted you access, sign out and back in.
