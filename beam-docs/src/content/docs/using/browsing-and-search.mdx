---
title: Browsing and searching
description: Find titles by browsing your libraries or with search, filters and sorting.
---

There are two ways to reach a title: browse a library, or search.

## Browsing

Each library the administrator has added appears as its own collection of titles. Opening one
lists everything indexed in it.

## Explore

The Explore view searches and filters across everything you can see. Filters available:

| Filter | Values |
|---|---|
| Type | Movie or show |
| Genre | Any genre present in your library |
| Year | A range, from and to |
| Minimum rating | 6, 7, 8 or 9 out of 10 |
| Sort by | Title, year or rating |
| Order | Ascending or descending |

Filters are reflected in the URL, so a filtered view is a link you can bookmark or share with
another user of the same server.

## Search

Search runs on the server and matches on **title similarity**, so a near-miss or a small typo
still finds the right thing. Results update as you type, with a short pause so that a fast typist
does not fire a query per keystroke.

Two things it does not do:

- It does **not** search inside descriptions, cast lists or subtitles — titles only.
- It only finds titles that have been **indexed**. If something is missing, the library may need
  a scan; ask an administrator, or see [Managing libraries](/operate/libraries/) if that is you.

## What you will not see

Beam never exposes filesystem paths through its API, so you cannot search by filename or
directory, and a title's page will not tell you where the file lives on disk.
