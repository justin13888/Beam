Your profile page shows the identity your provider asserted and every session currently signed in
as you.

## Your details

Name and email come from your identity provider and are refreshed when you sign in. Beam does not
let you edit them — change them at the provider.

Whether you are an admin is also shown, and is likewise not editable in Beam. It comes from a
claim your provider asserts, recalculated at every sign-in.

## Active sessions

Each session lists:

- a **device fingerprint**, so you can tell sessions apart without Beam storing your user agent
  verbatim,
- the **IP address it was created from** — this is recorded once at sign-in and is not updated
  as the session is used,
- when it was **created**, and when it was **last active**.

Revoke any one of them individually — useful if you signed in somewhere you no longer control.

## Signing out everywhere

There is also a sign-out-everywhere action. It ends **all** your sessions, including the one you
are using, so you will be signed out immediately and have to sign in again.

<Aside type="tip">
Reach for this if you think someone else has access to your account. Because Beam holds no
password, there is nothing else to rotate on Beam's side — but change your credentials at your
identity provider too, since that is what actually grants access.
</Aside>

## Session expiry

Sessions expire on their own: one after a period of inactivity, and every session at a fixed
maximum age regardless of use. The server's administrator sets both, so how long you stay signed
in varies between deployments. Being signed out unexpectedly is usually one of these rather than
a fault.
